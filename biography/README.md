# Biography / Biografie

## English

This section documents my personal journey in computer technology, from my early experiences with the Commodore C64 and Amiga systems to my involvement in hardware and software development.
I collect memories, experiences and background information about my projects, my technical interests and my contributions to the retro-computing community.
